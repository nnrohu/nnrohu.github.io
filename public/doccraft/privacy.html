Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2025

Overview

DocCraft is developed by Legit. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

DocCraft is a comprehensive document and image processing studio. The app helps you prepare passport photos, resize images, create signatures, and work with PDF and Office documents.

On-Device Processing

All photo, signature, and PDF processing in DocCraft happens entirely on your device. Your images and documents are never transmitted to external servers for on-device features. Processing is performed locally using your device's hardware, ensuring your personal documents remain private and under your control at all times.

Permissions

DocCraft requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera — Used to take passport photos directly within the app. Photos captured are processed on-device and are not uploaded or shared.
  • Photo Library Read — Used to import existing photos from your gallery for processing (resizing, cropping, background removal). Imported photos remain on your device.
  • Photo Library Write — Used to save processed images (resized photos, cropped signatures, generated PDFs) back to your device gallery.

These permissions are used solely for document processing purposes. No data accessed through these permissions is collected, stored on servers, or shared with third parties.

No Data Collection

For all on-device features, DocCraft does not collect, store, or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind. No usage data, device identifiers, or personal information is transmitted from the app.

Your photos, signatures, and documents are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party service when using on-device features.

Cloud-Based Features (Future)

DocCraft may include optional cloud-based premium features in the future, including:

  • Background Remover (BG Remover)
  • Word to PDF conversion
  • Excel to PDF conversion
  • PDF to Word conversion

When you use these cloud features, your files will be sent to a secure server for processing. Files are processed immediately and deleted from the server upon completion. No copies are retained, and no file content is used for any purpose other than performing the requested conversion.

Cloud features will always be clearly identified in the app, and you will be informed before any data leaves your device.

Data Deletion & GDPR Compliance

If account-based features are introduced in the future, you will have the right to request deletion of any data associated with your account. We will comply with all applicable data protection regulations, including GDPR, and will process deletion requests promptly.
